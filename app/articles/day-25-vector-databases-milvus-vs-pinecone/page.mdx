# Day 25 — Vector Databases: Milvus vs Pinecone

Vector databases power retrieval in RAG systems. Choosing between Milvus (self-hosted) and Pinecone (managed) depends on scale, security, and operational constraints.

## Quick comparison

- **Milvus (self-hosted):** Full control, no vendor lock-in, potentially lower long-term cost at scale, but requires operations expertise.
- **Pinecone (managed):** Fast time-to-market, SLA-backed performance, and simpler developer experience; potentially higher recurring costs.

### Key factors to evaluate

- **Throughput & latency:** Both can be tuned; benchmark with realistic shard counts and vector sizes.
- **Security & compliance:** Milvus gives you control for on-prem deployments; Pinecone provides VPC peering and encryption-in-transit options.
- **Multi-tenancy:** Pinecone offers multi-tenant abstractions; Milvus requires you to implement tenant isolation.

## Operational checklist

1. Simulate concurrent queries at expected QPS with representative vectors.
2. Measure index build times and reindexing windows for nightly updates.
3. Validate backup, restore, and disaster recovery procedures.

## Example: hybrid approach

Start with Pinecone for prototyping to reduce ops overhead. Migrate to Milvus for on-prem or cost-optimized production after you validate access patterns and scale.

## Benchmarks to collect

- recall@k on a representative corpus
- 95th/99th percentile latency for common vector sizes
- cost per million queries (approx)
