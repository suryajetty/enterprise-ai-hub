# Day 81 — Explaining AI Risk to a Non-Technical CISO

This note is a concise, non-technical briefing for CISOs who must evaluate AI initiatives. Focus on outcomes, not models.

## Key risks

- **Data leakage:** sensitive data could be exposed in prompts or model outputs.
- **Compliance risk:** models may produce responses that violate regulations.
- **Operational risk:** outages or cost spikes can cause business impact.

## Controls to ask for

1. Access controls and encryption for data-in-flight and at-rest.
2. Audit trails for prompts, retrievals, and actions taken.
3. Regular model evaluation and human-in-the-loop for high-risk domains.

## Board-level metrics

- Number of high-risk flows with HITL
- Monthly token spend trend
- Incidents attributable to model outputs
