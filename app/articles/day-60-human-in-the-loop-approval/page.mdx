# Day 60 — Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Approval Patterns

Human-in-the-loop is essential when agentic decisions have high business impact or regulatory risk. HITL should be a first-class control in any agentic workflow.

## Patterns

- **Pre-approval:** hold actions until a human approves (use for high-risk tasks).
- **Post-hoc review:** perform action immediately but queue the decision for review and reversal if needed.
- **Consensus:** require multiple reviewers for sensitive actions.

## UX considerations

Design approvals to minimize reviewer load: show the short rationale, suggested action, evidence (retriever hits), and a one-click approve/deny.

## Example flow

1. Agent generates a proposal and sets `risk_score`.
2. If `risk_score` > threshold, route to a human approver UI with conversation context.
3. On approval, the agent executes and logs the action with the approver ID.

## Auditability

Record the prompt, retriever context, decision rationale, and reviewer verdicts. Store these in an immutable log for compliance.
