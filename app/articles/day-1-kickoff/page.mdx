# Day 1 — The Kickoff: Announcement & Mission

Welcome to the 100-day Enterprise AI Blueprint. Over the next 100 days we’ll translate classical middleware and platform engineering patterns into production-ready AI workflows.

This series is for architects, platform engineers, and middleware practitioners who are migrating existing systems to AI-first designs. Each post is concise, prescriptive, and includes architectures, operational checklists, and code snippets you can re-use.

## What to expect

- Five phases covering architecture, RAG, agentic workflows, LLMOps, and organizational change.
- Twice-weekly practical essays with a downloadable blueprint at the end of each phase.
- Concrete artifacts: diagrams, code snippets, and a final compiled PDF.

## Mission

Teach engineers to stop treating models as black boxes. Instead, design observable, testable, and auditable AI systems that fit enterprise SLAs and compliance constraints.

## Quick checklist (first 7 days)

1. Inventory system-critical integrations and data sources.
2. Identify privacy-sensitive domains and regulatory constraints.
3. Prototype a small RAG flow with logging and synthetic tests.
