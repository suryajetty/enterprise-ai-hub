# Day 95 — My Biggest Failure Deploying AI to Production

This is a condensed postmortem of a deployment that caused significant customer impact due to insufficient guardrails and monitoring.

## What happened

- An automated agent made a high-impact change (billing adjustment) based on an ambiguous instruction.
- The change propagated to multiple systems before humans noticed.

## Root causes

1. No human-in-the-loop for high-risk actions.
2. Missing idempotency and unsafe retries.
3. Insufficient audit trail to roll back quickly.

## Corrective actions

- Introduced mandatory pre-approval for billing adjustments.
- Added an immutable audit log of prompts, retriever context, and actions.
- Implemented canary rollouts and immediate rollback playbooks.

The core lesson: treat agents like autonomous services with SRE playbooks. I can expand this into a full postmortem template for your team.
